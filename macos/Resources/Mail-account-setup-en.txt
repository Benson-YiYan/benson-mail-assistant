Email Assistant: step-by-step email setup
For Windows, Apple silicon Mac and Intel Mac. Sources checked: 2026-10-17.
Website labels can change; follow the current official page and your administrator's rules.

1. Start here
On first launch, use Connect email. Later, open Settings > Email on Mac or Email and checks on Windows. For another account, choose Add email account.
Enter your full email address. Enter only the IMAP hostname, without https://, a port or path. The presets below use port 993 with SSL/TLS. Leave Login name empty to use the full email address unless your provider says otherwise. The usual folder is INBOX.
Gmail and Outlook/Microsoft 365 use browser sign-in; users do not enter OAuth tokens or app-registration details. Other providers may require a provider-issued app password or client authorization code. These are not SMS verification codes. If a provider requires OAuth2 but the app has no browser sign-in option for it, that provider is not currently supported.
For Gmail and Outlook/Microsoft 365, click the browser sign-in button and approve on the provider’s official page. For other providers, enter the credential they require, save the email settings (or Save and continue), then test the connection. Pause monitoring before changing connection details.
Your email credential is separate from your AI API Key. Never post either in a chat. AI analysis must also be configured; no new messages means no AI calls.
If you already have AI software, ask it to help you obtain the authorization code.
Use that help on the provider's official site. Do not paste credentials or access tokens into an AI chat, and complete identity verification yourself.

Proxy connections (for example, Gmail from mainland China)
1) Start your proxy app, such as v2rayN, and verify that its selected server works. Webmail working does not prove that IMAP is using the proxy.
2) Select the email account in settings and on Windows expand Advanced connection and proxy options; on Mac find Mail proxy. Existing accounts default to Direct.
3) Try System proxy to read the actual system/environment proxy. An unavailable system proxy produces an error; it never silently switches to Direct.
4) Alternatively choose HTTP proxy or SOCKS5 proxy to match your proxy app. The host is usually 127.0.0.1. Check the actual local listening port in your proxy app; do not copy an example blindly. 10808 is only a common example.
5) Save email settings, then Test email connection, and resume monitoring after success. Each account has separate proxy settings. A custom proxy does not require TUN mode.
6) This feature proxies IMAP email connections only; it does not change AI API networking. PAC scripts and proxies requiring a username/password are not supported. Proxy failure produces an error instead of falling back to a direct connection.


Languages and language packs

The interface, installer and uninstaller support **Simplified Chinese, English and Spanish**. First use selects Chinese for Chinese system languages, Spanish for Spanish system languages and English otherwise. You can change it in General settings. Windows Setup offers **Download Simplified Chinese**, **Download English** and **Download Spanish**.

Only installed language packs can be used. Choosing a missing language asks “You have not downloaded this language. Download it now?” Confirm to download; cancelling or a failed download keeps the current language. New reports use the selected language with the same six analysis requirements; existing reports are not translated. The product name is **Email Assistant** in English and **Asistente de correo electrónico** in Spanish.


2. NetEase 163, 126 and Yeah
Official sign-in: https://mail.163.com/ , https://mail.126.com/ , https://www.yeah.net/ . Official help: https://help.mail.163.com/ .
1) Sign in to your provider's official webmail.
2) In settings, find POP3/SMTP/IMAP or client settings and enable IMAP. Enabling POP3 alone is insufficient.
3) Follow the site's security verification and generate a client authorization code. Enabling the service and adding a code may be combined on the current page. Complete any required phone verification. If the option is missing, consult official help or NetEase support.
4) Save the generated code securely; it may only be shown once. Revoke and regenerate a lost code on the official site.
5) In Email Assistant select the matching preset, enter the full email address and authorization code, and choose Password / app password. Servers: 163 = imap.163.com; 126 = imap.126.com; Yeah = imap.yeah.net. Use 993, SSL/TLS.
6) Save and test. Resolve account security restrictions and IMAP activation in webmail first. Your normal account password does not replace a required client authorization code.

3. QQ Mail / Foxmail addresses belonging to QQ Mail
Official webmail: https://mail.qq.com/ . Authorization-code help: https://service.mail.qq.com/detail/0/75 .
1) Sign in to QQ webmail. Open Settings > Account.
2) Find the POP3/IMAP/SMTP/Exchange/CardDAV/CalDAV services section and enable IMAP/SMTP.
3) Complete the site's verification and generate an authorization code. Follow the current page if the management entry has moved.
4) Select QQ / Foxmail in the app. Enter your full QQ address or an enabled @foxmail.com address belonging to that QQ mailbox. Use imap.qq.com, 993, SSL/TLS, Password / app password; paste the authorization code.
5) Save and test. Foxmail is also the name of a mail client: a company email account read inside Foxmail does not thereby use QQ servers. Choose its actual provider or Other email instead.

4. Tencent Exmail
Official sign-in: https://exmail.qq.com/ . Official client configuration: https://hiflow.tencent.com/document/applications/tencent-exmail/ .
1) Sign in to company webmail. Confirm IMAP and third-party clients are permitted; contact your administrator if not.
2) Look in account/security/client settings for your organization's login requirements. Where secure client login requires a dedicated client password, complete verification and generate one. Menu names vary by organization and version; do not assume personal QQ instructions apply.
3) Select Tencent Exmail. Enter the full company address, imap.exmail.qq.com, 993, SSL/TLS, Password / app password. Use the dedicated password when required; use a normal password only if your administrator explicitly permits it.
4) Save and test. Organization restrictions on IMAP, third-party clients or password authentication require administrator action.

5. Gmail
Official webmail: https://mail.google.com/ . Google OAuth information: https://developers.google.com/identity/protocols/oauth2/native-app .

Email Assistant uses browser sign-in with the Gmail API. Users do not create a Google OAuth app or enter a client ID, client secret, tenant or access token. Mail retrieval uses read-only access; sending uses a separate permission and happens only after you review and explicitly confirm a message.
Automatic summaries, manual email questions and reply drafts send this Gmail account's message bodies and readable attachment text to the AI service you selected. Each Gmail account has its own consent switch in mailbox settings. Without consent, its message content is not sent to AI. You can turn consent off at any time to stop future transfers.
1) Sign in to Gmail on the web first and make sure the account works.
2) In Email Assistant, choose the Gmail preset, enter the complete Gmail address and click **Sign in with Google**.
3) The browser opens Google’s official sign-in and consent page. Sign in to the mailbox, review the app name, account and permissions, and approve only when they match your expectations. The signed-in account must match the address in the app.
4) Google shows separate read-only mail and send permissions. Review them before approval. The app does not use the Gmail API to modify or delete mail.
5) Return to Email Assistant, click **Save email settings**, then **Test email connection**. Credentials are stored in the macOS Keychain or Windows protected credential storage and renewed when needed.

If Google shows an unverified-app warning or unexpected permissions, do not approve immediately; check the app name, account and scope. Revoked consent, Google policy changes or organization restrictions may require sign-in again.

6. iCloud Mail
Official webmail: https://www.icloud.com/mail/ . App-password help: https://support.apple.com/102654 . Server settings: https://support.apple.com/102525 .
1) Sign in at https://account.apple.com/ . Your Apple Account must have two-factor authentication enabled.
2) Open Sign-In and Security > App-Specific Passwords. Choose Generate an app-specific password or the plus button, name it Email Assistant, and complete verification.
3) Select iCloud in the app. Enter your actual iCloud email address and generated password. Use imap.mail.me.com, 993, SSL/TLS, Password / app password.
4) Apple says the IMAP username is usually the part before @. If the full email address fails, enter that part under the advanced Login name option and test again. For aliases/custom domains follow the valid account identity and Apple's requirements.
5) Save and test. Resetting the Apple Account main password revokes all app-specific passwords. Generate a new one; do not substitute the main password.

7. Yahoo Mail
Official webmail: https://mail.yahoo.com/ . App-password help: https://help.yahoo.com/kb/SLN15241.html . IMAP settings: https://help.yahoo.com/kb/SLN4075.html .
1) Sign in and open Account Security > Generate and manage app passwords.
2) Follow the page to name the app Email Assistant and generate/copy its password.
3) Select Yahoo in the app. Enter the full address, imap.mail.yahoo.com, 993, SSL/TLS, Password / app password, and the generated password. Save and test.
4) Some accounts may temporarily be unable to generate app passwords. Follow Yahoo's official instructions or contact support; availability is not guaranteed. SMS verification codes are not app passwords.

8. Outlook.com / Hotmail / Microsoft 365: browser sign-in
Personal webmail: https://outlook.live.com/mail/ . Work/school webmail: https://outlook.office.com/mail/ . Microsoft IMAP OAuth information: https://learn.microsoft.com/exchange/client-developer/legacy-protocols/how-to-authenticate-an-imap-pop-smtp-application-by-using-oauth .

Email Assistant provides browser sign-in for Outlook/Microsoft 365. Users do not register a Microsoft app or enter a client ID, client secret, tenant or access token.
1) For a personal Outlook.com mailbox, enable IMAP in webmail: Settings > Mail > Forwarding and IMAP > Let devices and apps use IMAP. For work/school mailboxes, contact the administrator if the option is missing and confirm IMAP and organization policies.
2) In Email Assistant, choose the Outlook/Microsoft 365 preset, enter the complete address and click **Sign in with Microsoft**.
3) The browser opens Microsoft’s official sign-in and consent page. Sign in to the target mailbox and review the app name and permissions before approving. The signed-in mailbox must match the address in the app.
4) Confirm `outlook.office365.com`, port `993` and SSL/TLS, then save and test the mailbox. Credentials are stored securely and renewed when needed.
5) Microsoft IMAP permission may allow reading, writing or deleting mail; Email Assistant only performs read operations and does not automatically send mail. Work/school mailboxes may require administrator approval or be restricted by organization policy.

Do not paste Microsoft Graph or Graph Explorer tokens into Email Assistant. Outlook sign-in is completed through the official browser flow.

9. Other / custom IMAP: the short version
1) Open your provider's own official webmail and sign in.
2) Find IMAP/third-party-client settings or official help and enable IMAP. If missing, contact the provider or company administrator. There is no universal menu or activation button.
3) Obtain the app password or client authorization code required by the provider. If the provider supports only OAuth2 and the app does not offer its browser sign-in, it cannot currently connect.
4) Return to Email Assistant, select Other email / Custom IMAP, and enter the full address, official IMAP hostname, port, encryption and credential. Set advanced Login name and Mail folder if required. Do not guess server names or use an SMTP sending server as IMAP.
5) Save, test, and start monitoring after success. If the provider specifies STARTTLS, use STARTTLS and its documented port. Providers offering only POP3, unsupported authentication, or no third-party access cannot currently connect.

10. Quick troubleshooting
Wrong server/port: check official IMAP settings; do not enter a web URL.
Authentication failed: check the full account address, the provider-required password/app password or authorization code, and whether browser sign-in needs to be completed again.
Company policy blocked access: contact the administrator.
No report for old messages: the first check records existing messages; reports begin with subsequent new messages.
