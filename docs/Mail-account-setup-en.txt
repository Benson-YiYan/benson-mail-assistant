Mail Secretary: step-by-step email setup
For Windows, Apple silicon Mac and Intel Mac. Sources checked: 2026-10-17.
Website labels can change; follow the current official page and your administrator's rules.

1. Start here
On first launch, use Connect email. Later, open Settings > Email on Mac or Email and checks on Windows. For another account, choose Add email account.
Enter your full email address. Enter only the IMAP hostname, without https://, a port or path. The presets below use port 993 with SSL/TLS. Leave Login name empty to use the full email address unless your provider says otherwise. The usual folder is INBOX.
There is no universal “IMAP code”. An app password / authorization code is generated by your email provider; it is not an SMS verification code. OAuth2 requires an access_token, not a refresh_token, id_token or authorization code.
Paste the required credential into Password / app password / OAuth2 token, choose the matching Authentication option, Save email settings (or Save and continue), then Test email connection. Pause monitoring before changing connection details.
Your email credential is separate from your AI API Key. Never post either in a chat. AI analysis must also be configured; no new messages means no AI calls.
If you already have AI software, ask it to help you obtain the authorization code.
Use that help on the provider's official site. Do not paste credentials or access tokens into an AI chat, and complete identity verification yourself.

2. NetEase 163, 126 and Yeah
Official sign-in: https://mail.163.com/ , https://mail.126.com/ , https://www.yeah.net/ . Official help: https://help.mail.163.com/ .
1) Sign in to your provider's official webmail.
2) In settings, find POP3/SMTP/IMAP or client settings and enable IMAP. Enabling POP3 alone is insufficient.
3) Follow the site's security verification and generate a client authorization code. Enabling the service and adding a code may be combined on the current page. Complete any required phone verification. If the option is missing, consult official help or NetEase support.
4) Save the generated code securely; it may only be shown once. Revoke and regenerate a lost code on the official site.
5) In Mail Secretary select the matching preset, enter the full email address and authorization code, and choose Password / app password. Servers: 163 = imap.163.com; 126 = imap.126.com; Yeah = imap.yeah.net. Use 993, SSL/TLS.
6) Save and test. Resolve account security restrictions and IMAP activation in webmail first. Your normal account password does not replace a required client authorization code.

3. QQ Mail / Foxmail addresses belonging to QQ Mail
Official webmail: https://mail.qq.com/ . Authorization-code help: https://service.mail.qq.com/detail/0/75 .
1) Sign in to QQ webmail. Open Settings > Account.
2) Find the POP3/IMAP/SMTP/Exchange/CardDAV/CalDAV services section and enable IMAP/SMTP.
3) Complete the site's verification and generate an authorization code. Follow the current page if the management entry has moved.
4) Select QQ / Foxmail in the app. Enter your full QQ address or an enabled @foxmail.com address belonging to that QQ mailbox. Use imap.qq.com, 993, SSL/TLS, Password / app password; paste the authorization code.
5) Save and test. Foxmail is also the name of a mail client: a company email account read inside Foxmail does not thereby use QQ servers. Choose its actual provider or Other email instead.

4. Tencent Exmail
Official sign-in: https://exmail.qq.com/ . Official client configuration: https://hiflow.tencent.com/document/applications/tencent-exmail/ .
1) Sign in to company webmail. Confirm IMAP and third-party clients are permitted; contact your administrator if not.
2) Look in account/security/client settings for your organization's login requirements. Where secure client login requires a dedicated client password, complete verification and generate one. Menu names vary by organization and version; do not assume personal QQ instructions apply.
3) Select Tencent Exmail. Enter the full company address, imap.exmail.qq.com, 993, SSL/TLS, Password / app password. Use the dedicated password when required; use a normal password only if your administrator explicitly permits it.
4) Save and test. Organization restrictions on IMAP, third-party clients or password authentication require administrator action.

5. Gmail
Official sign-in: https://mail.google.com/ . Account security: https://myaccount.google.com/security . App-password help: https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/185833?hl=en .
1) Sign in to your Google account, open Security and enable 2-Step Verification.
2) Open https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords , sign in again if asked, and create an app password named Mail Secretary.
3) Paste the generated 16-digit app password into the app. Choose Gmail, full email address, imap.gmail.com, 993, SSL/TLS, Password / app password. Save and test. Do not use your ordinary Google password.
4) App passwords may be unavailable for security-key-only 2-Step Verification, Advanced Protection, or administrator-managed work/school accounts. The app currently has no Google web OAuth sign-in. Ask your administrator or use a supported account if app passwords are unavailable; do not disable account protection to work around this.
5) Changing your Google account password revokes app passwords; generate a replacement afterward.

6. iCloud Mail
Official webmail: https://www.icloud.com/mail/ . App-password help: https://support.apple.com/102654 . Server settings: https://support.apple.com/102525 .
1) Sign in at https://account.apple.com/ . Your Apple Account must have two-factor authentication enabled.
2) Open Sign-In and Security > App-Specific Passwords. Choose Generate an app-specific password or the plus button, name it Mail Secretary, and complete verification.
3) Select iCloud in the app. Enter your actual iCloud email address and generated password. Use imap.mail.me.com, 993, SSL/TLS, Password / app password.
4) Apple says the IMAP username is usually the part before @. If the full email address fails, enter that part under the advanced Login name option and test again. For aliases/custom domains follow the valid account identity and Apple's requirements.
5) Save and test. Resetting the Apple Account main password revokes all app-specific passwords. Generate a new one; do not substitute the main password.

7. Yahoo Mail
Official webmail: https://mail.yahoo.com/ . App-password help: https://help.yahoo.com/kb/SLN15241.html . IMAP settings: https://help.yahoo.com/kb/SLN4075.html .
1) Sign in and open Account Security > Generate and manage app passwords.
2) Follow the page to name the app Mail Secretary and generate/copy its password.
3) Select Yahoo in the app. Enter the full address, imap.mail.yahoo.com, 993, SSL/TLS, Password / app password, and the generated password. Save and test.
4) Some accounts may temporarily be unable to generate app passwords. Follow Yahoo's official instructions or contact support; availability is not guaranteed. SMS verification codes are not app passwords.

8. Outlook.com / Hotmail / Microsoft 365: OAuth2
Personal webmail: https://outlook.live.com/mail/ . Work/school: https://outlook.office.com/mail/ .
Use OAuth2, not an account password or app password. Exchange Online removed IMAP basic authentication. Microsoft Graph tokens from Graph Explorer are not IMAP tokens. Mail Secretary accepts a manually obtained access_token; it does not perform Microsoft web sign-in or refresh tokens automatically.
A. Enable IMAP
1) In personal Outlook webmail, open Settings > Mail > Forwarding and IMAP and enable IMAP. Search Settings for IMAP if labels differ.
2) For Microsoft 365, ask your administrator to confirm IMAP is enabled and the application/delegated permission below is allowed. Admin consent or device-code restrictions may apply.
B. Register your own application once (IT assistance may be useful)
3) You need access to a Microsoft Entra directory with permission to register applications; a personal mailbox alone does not guarantee this. Ask IT for help if registration is unavailable. Sign in at https://entra.microsoft.com/ . Open Identity > Applications > App registrations > New registration. Name it Mail Secretary; choose an account type matching your mailbox. Personal Outlook requires a type including personal Microsoft accounts. Record the Application (client) ID.
4) In Authentication > Advanced settings, enable Allow public client flows. Do not create a client secret: these instructions use a public-client device flow.
5) Under API permissions > Add a permission > APIs my organization uses, find Office 365 Exchange Online. Select Delegated permissions and IMAP.AccessAsUser.All. Obtain administrator consent if required. Microsoft Graph Mail.Read is not the required permission.
C. Get a token on your own computer
6) On Windows, open PowerShell and paste the script below. Replace CLIENT-ID-HERE with your application ID. For personal accounts keep consumers; for an organization-only application replace it with your tenant ID. Do not enter an email password in the script. Mac users can ask their administrator to obtain a token or use PowerShell if already installed.

WHAT DOES THE FOLLOWING CODE DO?
This helper obtains a Microsoft email OAuth2 access token (access_token). It applies only to the Outlook / Hotmail / Microsoft 365 section. Users of 163, QQ or other providers can skip this section.
It asks Microsoft to start a sign-in and displays the official sign-in URL and a one-time code. After you sign in and approve access in your browser, it waits for Microsoft's response and copies the access_token to your clipboard. Paste that value into Mail Secretary's OAuth2 access token field, save, and test.
First complete the app registration and permission steps above. Replace CLIENT-ID-HERE with your own Application (client) ID; this is not your email address, mailbox password or AI API key. The default consumers tenant is for personal Microsoft accounts. For an organization account, use your tenant ID as described above; the app must also support that account type.
This code does not bypass sign-in or administrator approval and does not keep Mail Secretary signed in permanently. Access tokens expire. The current app does not refresh them automatically, so obtain a new token and update it when needed. Do not paste the resulting token into a chat.

$clientId = 'CLIENT-ID-HERE'
$tenant = 'consumers'
$scope = 'https://outlook.office.com/IMAP.AccessAsUser.All offline_access'
$base = "https://login.microsoftonline.com/$tenant/oauth2/v2.0"
$device = Invoke-RestMethod -Method Post -Uri "$base/devicecode" -Body @{client_id=$clientId;scope=$scope}
Write-Host $device.message
$until = (Get-Date).AddSeconds([int]$device.expires_in)
$token = $null
while ((Get-Date) -lt $until) {
    Start-Sleep -Seconds ([int]$device.interval)
    try {
        $token = Invoke-RestMethod -Method Post -Uri "$base/token" -Body @{grant_type='urn:ietf:params:oauth:grant-type:device_code';client_id=$clientId;device_code=$device.device_code}
        break
    } catch {
        try { $failure = $_.ErrorDetails.Message | ConvertFrom-Json } catch { throw }
        if ($failure.error -eq 'authorization_pending') { continue }
        if ($failure.error -eq 'slow_down') { $device.interval = [int]$device.interval + 5; continue }
        throw
    }
}
if (-not $token.access_token) { throw 'Sign-in expired; start again.' }
$token.access_token | Set-Clipboard
Write-Host 'Access token copied. Paste it only into Mail Secretary.'

7) Follow the Microsoft sign-in URL and one-time code shown in the terminal. Sign in as the mailbox to monitor and consent. Leave PowerShell running. Verify the application is your own registered app, and do not give the device code to someone else.
8) Once copied, select Outlook / Microsoft 365 in Mail Secretary. Enter the full email address, outlook.office365.com, 993, SSL/TLS. Choose OAuth2 access token and paste the clipboard into the credential field. Save and test; then replace the clipboard by copying ordinary text.
9) Tokens expire according to Microsoft/organization policy. Repeat steps 6–8 to renew the access token manually. offline_access can also return a refresh_token, which this app does not accept or refresh. Do not share token responses.
10) For consent/admin/permission/policy failures, ask your administrator to check registration, Exchange Online delegated permission, user consent and Conditional Access. There is no permanent token to buy.

9. Other / custom IMAP: the short version
1) Open your provider's own official webmail and sign in.
2) Find IMAP/third-party-client settings or official help and enable IMAP. If missing, contact the provider or company administrator. There is no universal menu or activation button.
3) Obtain the app password/authorization code required by the provider. If OAuth2 is mandatory, ask your administrator for an access_token valid for that IMAP service. The app has no universal OAuth sign-in or automatic refresh.
4) Return to Mail Secretary, select Other email / Custom IMAP, and enter the full address, official IMAP hostname, port, encryption and credential. Set advanced Login name and Mail folder if required. Do not guess server names or use an SMTP sending server as IMAP.
5) Save, test, and start monitoring after success. If the provider specifies STARTTLS, use STARTTLS and its documented port. Providers offering only POP3, unsupported authentication, or no third-party access cannot currently connect.

10. Quick troubleshooting
Wrong server/port: check official IMAP settings; do not enter a web URL.
Authentication failed: check account, authentication type, revoked app password or expired access token.
Company policy blocked access: contact the administrator.
No report for old messages: the first check records existing messages; reports begin with subsequent new messages.
