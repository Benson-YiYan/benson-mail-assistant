适用于邮件小秘书 macOS 和 Windows 版本。 / Applies to Mail Assistant for macOS and Windows.

1. 使用范围 / Scope

Mail Assistant（邮件小秘书）是用于 macOS 和 Windows 的邮件阅读、总结、问答和回复辅助工具。请仅连接你拥有或获授权使用的邮箱，并遵守适用法律及邮箱和 AI 服务商的规则。

Mail Assistant is a desktop tool for reading, summarizing, discussing and replying to email on macOS and Windows. Connect only mailboxes you own or are authorized to use, and follow applicable laws and your mail and AI providers’ rules.

2. 账户与授权 / Accounts and authorization

请妥善保管电脑账户、应用专用密码、授权码和 OAuth 授权。你可以暂停监听、移除邮箱，并在 Google、Microsoft 等服务商账户设置中撤销授权。移除邮箱或撤销授权不会自动删除已经保存在本机的报告和缓存。

Protect your computer account, app passwords and OAuth authorizations. You can pause monitoring, remove a mailbox and revoke access in your provider’s account settings. Removing an account or revoking access does not automatically delete reports or caches already saved locally.

3. AI 输出与发信 / AI output and sending

AI 总结、问答和回复草稿可能遗漏内容或产生错误。请核对原邮件、附件、收件人、金额、日期及重要事项，再据此行动。软件只会在你确认发送后提交回复；发送后能否撤回取决于邮箱服务商。

AI summaries, answers and reply drafts may contain errors or omissions. Check original messages, attachments, recipients, amounts, dates and important details before acting. The app submits replies only after your confirmation; recall availability depends on your mail provider.

Mail, AI, Codex, DeepSeek Harness and download services are provided by third parties under their own terms and privacy policies. Your chosen AI provider may charge for usage. Local cost estimates are informational; provider billing controls actual charges. Third-party availability and free allowances are not guaranteed by this app.

5. 数据、隐私与备份 / Data, privacy and backups

执行 AI 功能时，相关邮件内容、附件文字和对话会发送给你配置的 AI 服务。请确保你有权处理并向该服务提供这些内容。报告和其他本地数据由你管理，请在删除、卸载或迁移前备份重要内容。具体数据处理方式见隐私政策。

AI features send relevant mail content, attachment text and conversation to your configured AI service. Make sure you are authorized to process and share that content with the service. You manage local reports and data; back up important material before deletion, uninstallation or migration. See the privacy policy for details.
