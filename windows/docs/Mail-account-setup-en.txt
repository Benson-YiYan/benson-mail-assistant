Mail Secretary: step-by-step email setup
For Windows, Apple silicon Mac and Intel Mac. Sources checked: 2026-10-17.
Website labels can change; follow the current official page and your administrator's rules.

1. Start here
On first launch, use Connect email. Later, open Settings > Email on Mac or Email and checks on Windows. For another account, choose Add email account.
Enter your full email address. Enter only the IMAP hostname, without https://, a port or path. The presets below use port 993 with SSL/TLS. Leave Login name empty to use the full email address unless your provider says otherwise. The usual folder is INBOX.
There is no universal “IMAP code”. An app password / authorization code is generated by your email provider; it is not an SMS verification code. OAuth2 requires an access_token, not a refresh_token, id_token or authorization code.
Paste the required credential into Password / app password / OAuth2 token, choose the matching Authentication option, Save email settings (or Save and continue), then Test email connection. Pause monitoring before changing connection details.
Your email credential is separate from your AI API Key. Never post either in a chat. AI analysis must also be configured; no new messages means no AI calls.
If you already have AI software, ask it to help you obtain the authorization code.
Use that help on the provider's official site. Do not paste credentials or access tokens into an AI chat, and complete identity verification yourself.

2. NetEase 163, 126 and Yeah
Official sign-in: https://mail.163.com/ , https://mail.126.com/ , https://www.yeah.net/ . Official help: https://help.mail.163.com/ .
1) Sign in to your provider's official webmail.
2) In settings, find POP3/SMTP/IMAP or client settings and enable IMAP. Enabling POP3 alone is insufficient.
3) Follow the site's security verification and generate a client authorization code. Enabling the service and adding a code may be combined on the current page. Complete any required phone verification. If the option is missing, consult official help or NetEase support.
4) Save the generated code securely; it may only be shown once. Revoke and regenerate a lost code on the official site.
5) In Mail Secretary select the matching preset, enter the full email address and authorization code, and choose Password / app password. Servers: 163 = imap.163.com; 126 = imap.126.com; Yeah = imap.yeah.net. Use 993, SSL/TLS.
6) Save and test. Resolve account security restrictions and IMAP activation in webmail first. Your normal account password does not replace a required client authorization code.

3. QQ Mail / Foxmail addresses belonging to QQ Mail
Official webmail: https://mail.qq.com/ . Authorization-code help: https://service.mail.qq.com/detail/0/75 .
1) Sign in to QQ webmail. Open Settings > Account.
2) Find the POP3/IMAP/SMTP/Exchange/CardDAV/CalDAV services section and enable IMAP/SMTP.
3) Complete the site's verification and generate an authorization code. Follow the current page if the management entry has moved.
4) Select QQ / Foxmail in the app. Enter your full QQ address or an enabled @foxmail.com address belonging to that QQ mailbox. Use imap.qq.com, 993, SSL/TLS, Password / app password; paste the authorization code.
5) Save and test. Foxmail is also the name of a mail client: a company email account read inside Foxmail does not thereby use QQ servers. Choose its actual provider or Other email instead.

4. Tencent Exmail
Official sign-in: https://exmail.qq.com/ . Official client configuration: https://hiflow.tencent.com/document/applications/tencent-exmail/ .
1) Sign in to company webmail. Confirm IMAP and third-party clients are permitted; contact your administrator if not.
2) Look in account/security/client settings for your organization's login requirements. Where secure client login requires a dedicated client password, complete verification and generate one. Menu names vary by organization and version; do not assume personal QQ instructions apply.
3) Select Tencent Exmail. Enter the full company address, imap.exmail.qq.com, 993, SSL/TLS, Password / app password. Use the dedicated password when required; use a normal password only if your administrator explicitly permits it.
4) Save and test. Organization restrictions on IMAP, third-party clients or password authentication require administrator action.

5. Gmail
Official sign-in: https://mail.google.com/ . Account security: https://myaccount.google.com/security . App-password help: https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/185833?hl=en .
1) Sign in to your Google account, open Security and enable 2-Step Verification.
2) Open https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords , sign in again if asked, and create an app password named Mail Secretary.
3) Paste the generated 16-digit app password into the app. Choose Gmail, full email address, imap.gmail.com, 993, SSL/TLS, Password / app password. Save and test. Do not use your ordinary Google password.
4) App passwords may be unavailable for security-key-only 2-Step Verification, Advanced Protection, or administrator-managed work/school accounts. The app currently has no Google web OAuth sign-in. Ask your administrator or use a supported account if app passwords are unavailable; do not disable account protection to work around this.
5) Changing your Google account password revokes app passwords; generate a replacement afterward.

6. iCloud Mail
Official webmail: https://www.icloud.com/mail/ . App-password help: https://support.apple.com/102654 . Server settings: https://support.apple.com/102525 .
1) Sign in at https://account.apple.com/ . Your Apple Account must have two-factor authentication enabled.
2) Open Sign-In and Security > App-Specific Passwords. Choose Generate an app-specific password or the plus button, name it Mail Secretary, and complete verification.
3) Select iCloud in the app. Enter your actual iCloud email address and generated password. Use imap.mail.me.com, 993, SSL/TLS, Password / app password.
4) Apple says the IMAP username is usually the part before @. If the full email address fails, enter that part under the advanced Login name option and test again. For aliases/custom domains follow the valid account identity and Apple's requirements.
5) Save and test. Resetting the Apple Account main password revokes all app-specific passwords. Generate a new one; do not substitute the main password.

7. Yahoo Mail
Official webmail: https://mail.yahoo.com/ . App-password help: https://help.yahoo.com/kb/SLN15241.html . IMAP settings: https://help.yahoo.com/kb/SLN4075.html .
1) Sign in and open Account Security > Generate and manage app passwords.
2) Follow the page to name the app Mail Secretary and generate/copy its password.
3) Select Yahoo in the app. Enter the full address, imap.mail.yahoo.com, 993, SSL/TLS, Password / app password, and the generated password. Save and test.
4) Some accounts may temporarily be unable to generate app passwords. Follow Yahoo's official instructions or contact support; availability is not guaranteed. SMS verification codes are not app passwords.

8. Outlook.com / Hotmail / Microsoft 365: complete OAuth2 instructions
Adding an Outlook/Microsoft 365 mailbox is extremely difficult and strongly discouraged. If you enjoy tinkering, the complete steps are below:

Official websites:
Personal webmail: https://outlook.live.com/mail/
Work/school webmail: https://outlook.office.com/mail/
Microsoft Entra admin center: https://entra.microsoft.com/
Azure account registration: https://azure.microsoft.com/pricing/purchase-options/azure-account

Before starting: Mail Secretary currently requires your own registered application and a manually obtained OAuth2 access_token. It has no built-in Microsoft web sign-in or automatic token renewal. Tokens expire, so you will need to sign in again. Your mailbox password, app password, application ID, tenant ID and access token are different values and cannot replace one another. A Microsoft Graph / Graph Explorer token cannot authenticate to IMAP.
For simply viewing several mailboxes, an email client with Microsoft sign-in is usually easier. Mail Secretary cannot directly read accounts or mail stored in another client. To keep using its analysis, an alternative is to enable forwarding in Outlook webmail to an address already monitored by Mail Secretary, retain a copy, and send a test message. Organization policy may block forwarding; also check that attachments arrive intact. Do not automatically forward the receiving mailbox back to Outlook, which could create a loop.

A. Check whether your mailbox permits IMAP
1) Sign in to the mailbox in your browser. In personal Outlook, open the Settings gear > Mail > Forwarding and IMAP, enable access for devices and apps using IMAP, and save. Search Settings for IMAP if the labels differ.
2) For Microsoft 365 work/school mail, ask the administrator to confirm IMAP is enabled for this mailbox and that the application, user consent and device-code sign-in below are permitted. Repeatedly obtaining tokens will not fix a policy restriction.

B. Obtain access to a directory where you can register an app
3) Open the Microsoft Entra admin center and sign in with the account that will manage the application. If you already have an organization directory, use the account menu > Switch directory to select one you can access. The app-management account and the mailbox account may differ, but the app must support the mailbox account type.
4) If a personal Outlook account encounters AADSTS50020, stating that the account does not exist in the Microsoft Services tenant, it cannot access that directory. ADIbizaUX / 74658136-14ec-4630-ad9b-26e160ff0fc6 in that error is Microsoft's portal application ID; do not enter it into Mail Secretary. Repeated sign-ins and Cookie changes cannot grant directory permissions. Use a directory you can access or ask an organization administrator for suitable app-registration permissions.
5) If you have no usable directory, visit the Azure account registration page above and check your eligibility for a Free account. Having an Outlook mailbox or signing in to Entra does not mean you have completed Azure registration.
   Registration order: choose Free account / Start free > sign in with your Microsoft account > fill in the official account form and verify your phone > complete card verification on Microsoft's official page > review the current free-account terms and submit. Stop and check if the page instead asks you to purchase, upgrade to a paid plan, or accept unexpected conditions.
   Standard Azure Free account registration generally asks for a phone and an eligible credit/debit card for identity verification; a temporary authorization may appear. Never send card details or verification codes to AI or enter them in Mail Secretary. Free account and pay-as-you-go are different options: read the current terms instead of upgrading to a paid plan just to follow this guide.
   After completing your first Azure account registration, return to Entra, sign in again and use the account menu > Switch directory to select the directory created during registration (often named Default Directory). Register the application there; you do not need another tenant. Tenant creation has eligibility restrictions: free/trial accounts cannot create additional Workforce tenants in the Entra admin center. If your first signup provides no usable directory or New registration is blocked, stop and ask an administrator for help, or use the forwarding alternative above.
6) Once inside a usable directory, open Entra ID (called Identity in some older interfaces) > App registrations. A missing or disabled New registration button, or a permissions error, requires administrator assistance. The Tenant ID on the portal home page is not an application ID.

C. Register an application once and configure delegated IMAP access
7) Open App registrations > New registration. Enter Mail Secretary as the name, then choose Supported account types for the mailbox:
   - Personal Outlook / Hotmail: Personal Microsoft accounts only, or an option including both organization directories and personal Microsoft accounts.
   - Microsoft 365 within one organization: Accounts in this organizational directory only (Single tenant) may be appropriate.
   - Personal mailboxes and different organizations: choose the option including any organizational directory and personal Microsoft accounts; organizations may still require administrator approval.
   This helper uses the device-code flow. Leave Redirect URI empty and click Register.
8) On this application's Overview page, copy Application (client) ID, in the form xxxxxxxx-xxxx-xxxx-xxxx-xxxxxxxxxxxx. This is the ID requested by the terminal tool. For work/school mail, also record the relevant Directory (tenant) ID. Do not copy Object ID or the ID of another application.
9) Open this application's Authentication > Advanced settings (some interfaces use Settings / Advanced settings), set Allow public client flows to Yes, and save. This tool requires no client secret or certificate. Do not type a mailbox password or client secret into the terminal.
10) Open API permissions > Add a permission > APIs my organization uses, search for Office 365 Exchange Online and select it. Choose Delegated permissions, expand IMAP, select IMAP.AccessAsUser.All, and click Add permissions. Do not select Microsoft Graph Mail.Read, Application permissions, or IMAP.AccessAsApp.
    The included helper automatically requests https://outlook.office.com/IMAP.AccessAsUser.All offline_access. You do not need to edit a script or copy PowerShell commands.
11) If administrator consent is required, ask the administrator to review and grant it. If the button is unavailable, ask for help; personal accounts generally consent during the later sign-in. An organization blocking the app or device-code flow must resolve that policy before proceeding.

D. Obtain an access token with the included helper
What it does: requests Microsoft device-code sign-in and displays an official URL and one-time code. After you approve access in your browser, it copies access_token to your clipboard. It does not print the full token, save a token file or ask for your mailbox password. Skip this section for providers such as 163 or QQ that do not require Microsoft's OAuth2.
12) In Mail Secretary, choose the Outlook / Microsoft 365 preset and OAuth2 access token authentication. Click Get OAuth2 Token beside How do I get an authorization code?
    Windows: this runs Get-Outlook-Token.bat in the installation folder, which calls the adjacent PowerShell script. Keep both files together; no manual command copying is needed.
    Mac: this opens the included Get-Outlook-Token.command and bundled token helper. You do not need to install PowerShell.
13) At Application (client) ID, paste the ID from step 8 and press Enter. Do not enter an email address, tenant ID, account password or AI API key.
14) At the Tenant prompt:
    - Personal Outlook / Hotmail: press Enter to use the default, consumers.
    - Work/school mail: enter the Directory (tenant) ID of the mailbox's organization and ensure the registered app supports it.
15) Open the official Microsoft sign-in URL shown by the helper (usually https://microsoft.com/devicelogin ) in your browser. Enter the one-time code shown by this run of the helper; it is neither your mailbox password nor the access token. Sign in as the mailbox you want to monitor, verify the app name and permissions, then approve. Do not use a code supplied by someone else.
16) Leave the terminal window open until it confirms Access token copied. If the code or sign-in expires, rerun the tool to obtain a new code.

E. Enter the token in Mail Secretary and test
17) Email address: the complete address of the mailbox you just authorized.
    IMAP server: outlook.office365.com
    Port: 993
    Encryption: SSL/TLS
    Authentication: OAuth2 access token
    Credential field: paste the copied access_token.
    Advanced connection settings > Login name: normally leave empty so it follows the email address; set it only if your administrator requires it.
18) Save, then click Test mailbox connection. Start monitoring after success. The first check only records a starting point; subsequent new mail generates reports. Send a new test message to check the full flow.
19) After saving, copy ordinary text to replace the clipboard. Do not paste the full token into a chat, screenshot or guide.
20) Access tokens expire, with a common default lifetime of 60-90 minutes; the actual lifetime depends on Microsoft's response and organization policy. Even though offline_access is requested, this helper does not save or use refresh tokens. Click the helper button again, sign in again and update the credential when needed. This direct Microsoft mailbox connection is therefore unsuitable for users expecting long-term unattended operation.

F. Troubleshooting: follow the error instead of repeatedly retrying
- AADSTS50020 / Microsoft Services: review steps 3-6 for directory access. You cannot add yourself to Microsoft's Microsoft Services tenant; select a directory you are allowed to access. If this occurs during token sign-in, also check the supported account types and tenant in steps 7 and 14.
- AADSTS50058 / Interaction required: first click Sign in again and explicitly choose your account. If it repeats, investigate browser sessions, Cookies and blocking extensions, or try a browser with a working signed-in session. If you temporarily disable Safari's Prevent cross-site tracking to troubleshoot, restore it afterward. That setting cannot fix AADSTS50020 directory permissions.
- Cannot find the app ID: open your own registered application's Overview and copy Application (client) ID. Do not use Tenant ID, Object ID or the portal's app ID from an error message.
- invalid_client / unauthorized_client: verify the client ID, supported account types and saved Allow public client flows setting.
- consent_required / Administrator approval required / access_denied: check delegated permissions and organization policy with the administrator; do not substitute an ordinary password.
- Token obtained but IMAP authentication fails: check IMAP is enabled, the token belongs to the same mailbox and has not expired, and the requested scope is Exchange Online IMAP rather than Microsoft Graph. Organization policy may still block access.

9. Other / custom IMAP: the short version
1) Open your provider's own official webmail and sign in.
2) Find IMAP/third-party-client settings or official help and enable IMAP. If missing, contact the provider or company administrator. There is no universal menu or activation button.
3) Obtain the app password/authorization code required by the provider. If OAuth2 is mandatory, ask your administrator for an access_token valid for that IMAP service. The app has no universal OAuth sign-in or automatic refresh.
4) Return to Mail Secretary, select Other email / Custom IMAP, and enter the full address, official IMAP hostname, port, encryption and credential. Set advanced Login name and Mail folder if required. Do not guess server names or use an SMTP sending server as IMAP.
5) Save, test, and start monitoring after success. If the provider specifies STARTTLS, use STARTTLS and its documented port. Providers offering only POP3, unsupported authentication, or no third-party access cannot currently connect.

10. Quick troubleshooting
Wrong server/port: check official IMAP settings; do not enter a web URL.
Authentication failed: check account, authentication type, revoked app password or expired access token.
Company policy blocked access: contact the administrator.
No report for old messages: the first check records existing messages; reports begin with subsequent new messages.
